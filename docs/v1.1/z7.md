# Z7 †: a second-order correction for attribution patching

† Added after the study, exploratory, defined in `analysis-plan-v1.1.md` (Z7 and its clarification of 2026-11-13) before it was run. It changes no preregistered decision. Mean over models with 95% bootstrap interval over models, seed 20261101.

Patching is the first-order change of a readout when one connection class is scaled to zero. The correction adds half the second derivative along the same class (the diagonal of the Hessian, one Hessian-vector product per class and readout direction), so the score is the second-order Taylor expansion of the ablation. Same readout directions and aggregation as patching.

**Conclusion †.** On the same 8 stimuli patching reaches 0.969 of the achievable signal and patching with the Hessian correction 0.971 (mean over 10 models; patching on all 40 stimuli 0.974); the AUROC changes by +0.0007 [-0.0009, +0.0021] (the interval of the AUROC change includes zero), which closes 5% of the gap to the ceiling.
